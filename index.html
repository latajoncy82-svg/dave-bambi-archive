ARCHIVE NOTES

Sources & methodology

Roster evidence

Each entry links to a character page or a named roster section. Mod badges use roster or character-page evidence. Profiles separate appearances, crossovers, and archived material. Popcorn Edition includes its documented cast. Standalone fan OCs can be browsed without assigning them to a mod.

Estimated popularity

Ranking combines an editorial spotlight for recognizable characters, documented mod coverage, and primary-roster prominence. Ties sort by name. It is an archive estimate and uses no traffic, download, or vote data. Recently Added follows archive ingestion order.

Artwork & credits

Portraits are tied to the character sections that identify them. File credits link back to the original source. Artwork hosted by the source needs an internet connection. The database, profiles, and filters are bundled locally.
